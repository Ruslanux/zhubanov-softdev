HR Tech Ready to deploy

HR platform with multi-agent artificial intelligence

Automation of the entire employee lifecycle: 16 AI agents cover hiring, onboarding, performance reviews, learning, engagement and compliance.

1,005automated tests
16AI agents under an orchestrator
8access roles, multi-tenant

01Challenge

HR teams spend most of their time on routine work: job descriptions, initial resume screening, interview scheduling, paperwork for new hires, collecting reviews and reports. The goal was a platform where AI agents do this work while people make the decisions — with control over model costs and protection of personal data.

02Solution

  • An orchestrator over 15 specialized agents: job descriptions, resume screening, candidate sourcing, interviews, documents, IT provisioning, payroll setup, analytics and attrition prediction
  • Bias detection in job descriptions and candidate evaluations
  • Performance review cycles: self-assessment, manager review, 360° feedback and AI summaries
  • A training catalog, role-based learning paths and skills-gap analysis
  • Engagement surveys with sentiment analysis, eNPS and risk areas; compliance monitoring
  • A public careers portal, e-signing of documents and an HR chatbot

03Results

  • Token and cost accounting per agent, budget checks before each run, fallback model chains
  • Multi-tenancy with isolated organization data and 8 access roles
  • Masking of sensitive data in logs, audit trail, switching model providers without rewriting code
  • 1,005 automated tests; security and UX reviews completed
