Social Ready to deploy

Platform for serious dating and starting a family

A dating platform built on family values: a five-stage system of dates guides a couple from meeting online to deciding to start a family.

5stages from meeting to decision
Real-timechat with per-user streams
PDFcompatibility reports

01Challenge

Most dating services are built for entertainment. The client needed a platform for people looking for a family: with a clear path from meeting to decision, safe communication and a premium design that reflects the national culture.

02Solution

  • A five-stage system of dates that guides the couple
  • Real-time chat with a personal stream for each participant
  • Compatibility reports in PDF
  • Payments through bank acquiring with webhook processing
  • Google sign-in, access control and an admin panel with analytics
  • A custom design system: matte surfaces, brass accents and a national ornament

03Results

  • Reliable message delivery: rendered correctly for sender and recipient, without duplicates
  • Automated tests for models, controllers and chat
  • A deployment guide and a brand book
  • Server, database and photos moved to a data centre in Almaty: user data is stored in Kazakhstan, with about 10 minutes of downtime during the move
