About

Zhubanov SoftDev — a full-cycle engineering team

We build custom software and implement applied artificial intelligence for businesses and the public sector in Kazakhstan and abroad.

Zhubanov SoftDev (Sole Proprietor Zhubanov R. Zh.) is a software development company. We build web services and backend systems, integrations and business-process automation, solutions based on large language models and agent pipelines, data processing, computer vision, geographic information systems and real-time systems.

Our portfolio includes education and e-commerce platforms, HR systems with multi-agent AI, fintech solutions, construction and BIM tools, field reference tools for industrial engineers, security and recognition systems, and modules for accounting and government systems.

We help companies automate routine operations, speed up workflows and make data-driven decisions. We deliver turnkey projects — discovery, design, development, testing, deployment and support — including documentation and training for the client's staff.
