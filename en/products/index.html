Qyzmet

AI agents — digital employees on top of your accounting system

Each agent handles its own process: it prepares a draft that cites its source and waits for an employee's decision. Numbers, rates, stock and limits are checked by code, not by the model. The agent never sends anything on its own — everything is logged.

  • Runs on top of 1C, ERP and WhatsApp
  • A source for every answer
  • A human makes the decision
