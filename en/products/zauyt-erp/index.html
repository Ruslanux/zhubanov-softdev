ERP for building-materials plants and quarries

Zauyt ERP

Output, shipments and customer money — in one system. The weighbridge will not ship beyond the order, the payment or the credit limit, and pit foremen's reports are reconciled with the weight received at the plant.

Request a demo All products

What it solves

ERP for building-materials plants and quarries

  • Cost per tonne is calculated once a month, after the fact
  • A truck leaves with a load beyond the payment or credit limit
  • Pit reports and plant weights disagree — and nobody investigates
  • Material use is not compared with the norm for the tonnes produced

Industry modules

Zauyt ERP

Products and output

Grades with plan, price and storage capacity; output from shift reports, shipments from the weighbridge, stock against capacity.

Budget and norms

Site budget from Excel with a norm per tonne; issued from stock vs. the norm; cost per tonne.

Weighbridge

Shipments by order and raw material intake; flags: number, tare, over the order, over payment or limit, repeat trip.

Sales

Customers with credit limits, orders with customer trucks, shipped and owed by weighbridge, payments.

Quarry, machinery and fuel

Pit foremen's reports vs. weight at the plant; engine hours, trips and fuel; consumption per engine hour vs. the norm.

Shared ERP core

Requests, procurement, warehouse, budget, timesheets, reports and exports are shared by every industry ERP.
