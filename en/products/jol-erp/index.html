ERP for carriers and forwarders

Jol ERP

Orders, trips and margin — in one system. A driver with an expiring medical certificate cannot be assigned to a trip, and downtime caused by the client above the norm goes straight into the order amount.

Request a demo All products

What it solves

ERP for carriers and forwarders

  • Order margin is known only after the month is closed
  • A driver leaves on a trip with an expired medical certificate or ADR licence
  • Downtime caused by the client is never invoiced — the hours are lost
  • From 1 January 2027 e-waybills in ESUTD are mandatory, while trips are tracked in spreadsheets

Industry modules

Jol ERP

Transport orders

Route, cargo, mode of transport, rate and planned cost; margin below 10% or over the credit limit — goes for approval.

Trips

Own truck and driver, or a hired carrier and rail tariff; departure, arrival, e-waybill, delays.

Driver permits

Licence, medical certificate, tachograph card, passport, ADR — a driver cannot be assigned if a permit expires before delivery.

Downtime

Hours and the party at fault; above the norm through the client's fault — added to the order amount with a notice to accounting.

Money and fleet

Margin from actual trips, branch revenue, receivables, reconciliation statement, e-invoices; cost per kilometre and fuel per 100 km.

Shared ERP core

Requests, procurement, warehouse, budget, timesheets, reports and exports are shared by every industry ERP.
