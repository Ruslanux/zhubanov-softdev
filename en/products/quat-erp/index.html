ERP for energy companies

Quat ERP

CHP fuel, grid losses and the investment programme — in one system. Excess fuel consumption and losses above the norm are shown in tonnes, MWh and tenge, and a calorific shortfall in coal becomes a claim against the supplier.

Request a demo All products

What it solves

ERP for energy companies

  • Excess fuel use by a boiler is noticed at the end of the quarter
  • Coal arrives below its certificate calorific value — and no claim is filed
  • Above-norm grid losses are calculated once a year
  • Investment programme progress is compiled from emails and spreadsheets

Industry modules

Quat ERP

Coal and weighbridge

Coal trips with calorific value by certificate and laboratory; shortfall claims; coal stock in days.

CHP fuel balance

Specific equivalent fuel consumption against the norm, overspend in tonnes and tenge for electricity and heat.

Grid energy balance

Energy into the grid, useful supply, losses against the norm; above the norm — in MWh and tenge.

Investment programme

Measures with physical volume, budget and progress; Excel export.

Work permits

Permits with checks of electrical safety groups and permit deadlines.

Shared ERP core

Requests, procurement, warehouse, budget, timesheets, reports and exports are shared by every industry ERP.
